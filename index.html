Concept · Signal School

Every signal has a
card for when it lies.

Beginners don't blow up because they don't know an indicator. They blow up because nobody told them when it stops working. Tap a card.

Momentum family

RSI

Measures how stretched recent gains are versus recent losses. Above 70 reads “overbought”.

What it measuresFlip ↻
When it lies

In a real trend.

Strong trends can sit “overbought” for weeks. Shorting every 70 print is how trend-followers get paid by you.

Quick check · +25 XP↻
Positioning family

Funding rate

What perp longs pay shorts (or the reverse). High positive funding means crowded longs.

What it measuresFlip ↻
When it lies

When crowds are right.

Crowded doesn't mean wrong. Funding can stay hot through an entire leg up — it tells you the cost of being early, not the top.

Quick check · +25 XP↻
Participation family

Volume spike

A burst of traded size versus the recent average. Often read as conviction behind a move.

What it measuresFlip ↻
When it lies

It's a liquidation.

A cascade of forced sells prints huge volume with zero conviction behind it. Check open interest before you call it “smart money”.
